SVMs & neural networks

12 scenario questions in the CertNexus style. Answer first, then open the explanation. Use as the end-of-day team quiz or for revision.

1In an SVM, support vectors are…

  • A. All training points
  • B. Points on or inside the margin that define the boundary
  • C. Outliers removed before training
  • D. Cluster centres
Show answer

B — Only these points determine the hyperplane.

2Increasing C in a soft-margin SVM…

  • A. Widens the margin
  • B. Penalises violations more → narrower margin, more variance
  • C. Has no effect
  • D. Changes the kernel
Show answer

B — High C = less tolerance of misclassification.

3The two key hyperparameters of an RBF SVM are…

  • A. k and distance
  • B. C and γ
  • C. Depth and leaves
  • D. Learning rate and epochs
Show answer

B — Tune them together on a log grid.

4Why are kernel SVMs rarely trained on millions of rows?

  • A. They need GPUs
  • B. Training cost grows roughly quadratically or worse with n
  • C. They cannot classify
  • D. They need one-hot data
Show answer

B — Use linear SVMs, SGD or kernel approximations (Nyström) instead.

5In SVR, errors inside the ε-tube…

  • A. Are penalised quadratically
  • B. Are ignored
  • C. Double the loss
  • D. Become support vectors
Show answer

B — ε defines a zone of tolerated error.

6Default activation for hidden layers in modern MLPs?

  • A. Sigmoid
  • B. ReLU
  • C. Softmax
  • D. Linear
Show answer

B — ReLU avoids saturation and trains fast; sigmoid/softmax belong in output layers.

7Output layer for classifying documents into 5 types?

  • A. 1 sigmoid unit
  • B. 5 softmax units
  • C. 5 ReLU units
  • D. 1 linear unit
Show answer

B — Mutually exclusive classes → softmax over 5.

8Backpropagation computes…

  • A. The forward output
  • B. Gradients of the loss w.r.t. every weight via the chain rule
  • C. The learning rate
  • D. Feature importance
Show answer

B — The optimiser then uses these gradients to update the weights.

9With 150,000 rows and batch size 512, one epoch contains about…

  • A. 1 update
  • B. 293 updates
  • C. 512 updates
  • D. 150,000 updates
Show answer

B — 150,000 / 512 ≈ 293 gradient steps.

10Training loss keeps falling while validation loss rises after epoch 5. Best action?

  • A. Train longer
  • B. Early stopping, dropout or L2
  • C. Raise the learning rate
  • D. Remove the validation set
Show answer

B — Classic over-fitting; regularise and keep the best-validation weights.

11Which architecture fits scanned cheque images best?

  • A. MLP on raw pixels
  • B. CNN
  • C. k-means
  • D. Linear regression
Show answer

B — Convolutions exploit spatial structure with shared weights.

12An MLP ties a logistic-regression churn champion on tabular data. Best decision?

  • A. Deploy the MLP — it is deep learning
  • B. Keep the simpler champion unless the challenger is materially better
  • C. Average them blindly
  • D. Retrain until it wins on test
Show answer

B — Complexity costs explainability, governance and compute; it must earn its place.
