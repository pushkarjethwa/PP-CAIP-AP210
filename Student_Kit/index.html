Horizon Gulf Bank — Project MIZAN

Welcome. For five days you are a data scientist on Project MIZAN, the AI platform of a fictional UAE bank. Everything you need is in this folder: five study guides, 16 hands-on labs, daily exam practice and a pre-assessment, all on realistic synthetic banking data.

Before Day 1

  1. Install the environment: follow Environment setup (pip install -r Setup/requirements.txt). Open notebooks from inside this Student_Kit folder: the first cell of every notebook finds data/ and checkpoints/ automatically.
  2. Take the pre-assessment (40 questions, 60 min). No pass mark: it shows which exam domains need the most attention. Use Copy summary to share your result with your trainer.
  3. Skim the Day 1 study guide.

How labs work

Level 1 · From scratch

Read the HTML lab guide and write every line yourself. Try it when you have finished early or already know the topic.

Level 2 · Guided starter — ★ your default

The scaffolding is written. Fill in the numbered ✏️ BLANKs; each one says what to write, where and why.

Level 3 · Complete solution

Not in this kit. Your trainer hands out the day's solutions after the last lab (see Solution releases).

Labs build on each other, but every hand-off file is already in checkpoints/. If you fall behind, you can still start the next lab on time.

Solution releases

At the end of each day your trainer shares DayN_Release.zip. Extract it into this Student_Kit folder and allow it to merge folders. The Level 3 solution notebooks land next to your own work. The Day 5 release also contains the post-assessment.

AssessmentWhenFormat
Pre-assessmentBefore Day 140 questions · 60 min · baseline
Post-assessment (Day 5 release)After Day 5, before you book the exam80 questions · 120 min · 60% pass mark · full AIP-210 simulation

Both are weighted like the CertNexus AIP-210 blueprint (v1.7) and score you by exam domain and course day. ≥ 70% on the post-assessment: book the exam. 60–69%: borderline, so revise your weakest domain first. < 60%: not yet. Re-work the labs and daily exam practice for your weak domains.
