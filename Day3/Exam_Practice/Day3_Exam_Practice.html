Classification, clustering & trees

12 scenario questions in the CertNexus style. Answer first, then open the explanation. Use as the end-of-day team quiz or for revision.

1Fraud is 0.4% of transactions. A model predicting ‘never fraud’ has accuracy…

  • A. 0.4%
  • B. 50%
  • C. 99.6%
  • D. Undefined
Show answer

C — The accuracy paradox: high accuracy, zero recall.

2Best single metric to compare fraud models under heavy imbalance?

  • A. Accuracy
  • B. PR-AUC (average precision)
  • C. R²
  • D. Inertia
Show answer

B — PR-AUC focuses on the positive class; its baseline is the positive rate.

3Lowering the decision threshold of a classifier generally…

  • A. Raises precision and recall
  • B. Raises recall, lowers precision
  • C. Lowers both
  • D. Changes AUC
Show answer

B — More positives predicted: catch more true positives with more false alarms. AUC is threshold-free.

4Why must features be scaled for k-NN?

  • A. To speed up training
  • B. Distances are dominated by large-unit features otherwise
  • C. To avoid over-fitting
  • D. k-NN requires normal data
Show answer

B — Salary in AED would swamp age in years.

5exp(β) for a feature in logistic regression is…

  • A. The probability
  • B. The odds ratio for a one-unit increase
  • C. The AUC
  • D. The Gini index
Show answer

B — Coefficients are log-odds changes; exponentiate for odds ratios.

6Silhouette ≈ 0.15 for customer segments means…

  • A. Perfect separation
  • B. Weak but possibly useful structure
  • C. k is too small
  • D. The data has no rows
Show answer

B — Behavioural data rarely forms crisp clusters; business usefulness also matters.

7In a dendrogram, cutting at a greater height gives…

  • A. More clusters
  • B. Fewer clusters
  • C. The same clusters
  • D. A decision tree
Show answer

B — Higher cuts merge more groups.

8Gini impurity of a node with 50% / 50% classes is…

  • A. 0
  • B. 0.25
  • C. 0.5
  • D. 1
Show answer

C — 1 − (0.5² + 0.5²) = 0.5, the maximum for two classes.

9A decision tree has training AUC 1.00 and validation AUC 0.53. Best fix?

  • A. Increase depth
  • B. Limit depth / min_samples_leaf or prune
  • C. Remove the validation set
  • D. Use accuracy
Show answer

B — Classic over-fitting; constrain complexity.

10Random forests reduce variance mainly through…

  • A. Boosting residuals
  • B. Bagging plus random feature subsets
  • C. Pruning a single tree
  • D. Scaling features
Show answer

B — Averaging decorrelated trees reduces variance.

11A regression tree trained on 2018–2024 prices predicts 2025 prices that are too low. Why?

  • A. Trees cannot extrapolate beyond the target range seen in training
  • B. Too few trees
  • C. The learning rate is wrong
  • D. Gini is biased
Show answer

A — Leaves predict training means; a rising trend beyond the range is invisible.

12The retention team can call 10% of customers. Which chart best shows the value of the model?

  • A. ROC curve
  • B. Cumulative gains / lift chart
  • C. Elbow plot
  • D. Residual plot
Show answer

B — Gains answer ‘what share of churners do we reach by calling the top x%?’
